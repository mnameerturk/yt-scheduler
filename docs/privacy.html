NMR Scheduler – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

NMR Scheduler is a private tool operated by Nameer Turk to upload and schedule videos to YouTube channels owned or managed by its operator. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the tool accesses through Google and YouTube API Services and how that data is handled.

Use of YouTube API Services

NMR Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access

  • Google account email address (openid, email scopes): to show which account is connected.
  • YouTube channel ID, name and profile picture (youtube scope): to show which channel a video will be posted to and to list that channel's playlists.
  • Video uploads (youtube.upload scope): to upload videos, set their thumbnail, and add them to a playlist chosen by the operator.
  • Google Drive files created by this tool (drive.file scope): videos are placed in a "NMR Scheduler" folder in Drive until they are posted. The tool cannot see any other Drive files.

How data is used

Data is used only to upload and schedule the operator's own videos. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not combined with data from other channels, and not used to calculate analytics or metrics.

Storage and sharing

Login tokens and the video queue (titles, descriptions, tags, times, upload status) are stored on a server controlled by the operator. They are not shared with any third party. Video files are deleted from Google Drive as soon as they are posted to YouTube. Queue records for posted videos are deleted or refreshed within 30 days.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove the tool's access at any time from your Google security settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing an account inside NMR Scheduler deletes its stored login tokens immediately. To ask for all stored data to be deleted, email the contact below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
