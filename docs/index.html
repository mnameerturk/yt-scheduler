Upload and schedule videos to my YouTube channels

NMR Scheduler is a private tool used by Nameer Turk to upload and schedule videos to his own YouTube channels. Finished videos are kept in Google Drive until their planned time, then uploaded to YouTube with their title, description, tags, thumbnail and playlist.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Sign in with Google to connect my YouTube channels and Google Drive
  • Upload finished videos from my PC to Drive once
  • Post them to YouTube at the scheduled time, with thumbnail and playlist
  • Delete the Drive copy after posting
